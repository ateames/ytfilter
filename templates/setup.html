Add YouTube Home to your device

Follow these steps to bookmark this page so you can always find it.

{% if device == "ios" %}
  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (box with arrow)
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Name it "YouTube Home" and tap Add

→ Result: App icon appears on your home screen

{% elif device == "android" %}
  1. Tap the three-dot menu in Chrome (top right)
  2. Tap "Add to Home screen"
  3. Name it "YouTube Home" and tap Add

→ Result: App icon appears on your home screen

{% else %}
  1. Bookmark this page (Ctrl+D / Cmd+D)
  2. Or drag the address bar URL to your bookmarks bar
  3. Consider setting {{ base_url }} as your browser homepage
{% endif %}
